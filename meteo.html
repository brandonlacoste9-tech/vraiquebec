Reading Quebec weather like a local

  • Wind is the story. On the St. Lawrence — Charlevoix, Percé, the Gaspé coast — wind chill and gusts matter more than the thermometer says.
  • Ski season runs late. Tremblant and the Laurentians often ski into April; check snowfall, not just temperature.
  • Summer storms build fast. A 40% precipitation chance in July usually means a short afternoon thunderstorm, not a ruined day.
  • Fall colours peak early up north. Saguenay and Charlevoix turn two to three weeks before Montréal — late September is the sweet spot.
